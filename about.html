About Us

OUR COMMUNITY

A shared heritage. A connected community.

Kumari District Hindu Nadar Sangam brings together the Hindu Nadar community in Kanyakumari district. The Sangam’s recorded history traces its roots to the Travancore Nadar Mahajana Sangam and its first conference on 9 January 1930.

The organisation was registered as a society under registration number 7/1993. Explore the original Tamil history to learn about its development and the people who helped shape it.
